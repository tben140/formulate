# Policy drafts: terms of service, shipping, accessibility

Drafts for Ben to read, change and paste into Shopify. Nothing here is live,
and none of it is legal advice: have the terms checked before the store takes
real orders.

**Where each goes**

- **Terms of service and Shipping policy:** Shopify admin → Settings →
  Policies.
- **Accessibility statement:** Online Store → Pages, as a page.

Then add all three to the menu under Content → Menus → Legal. Web, the app
and the theme all read that menu, so the links appear on all three surfaces
with no code change.

**Placeholders to fill:** `[business name]`, `[business address]`,
`[contact email]` and `[company number]`. Use a business address, not a home
one; a PO box or virtual office is fine.

**Written to match what the store does today:**

- delivery is £3.95, and free on orders of £40 or more;
- subscriptions ship free;
- the refund policy has a 30-day money-back guarantee;
- subscriptions can be skipped, paused or cancelled at any time.

If any of those change, these drafts need to change with them.

## Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to shop with Double Helix, including people who
use a screen reader, a keyboard, voice control or zoom.

### What we aim for

Our website, our app and our Shopify theme aim to meet the Web Content
Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

### How we check

- **Automated scans:** changes to the website are scanned against WCAG 2.2
  AA with axe in our automated tests, on desktop and phone sizes. `[The
theme's scans arrive with #59.]`
- **Keyboard tests:** automated tests check that browsing, choosing options
  and adding to the basket work with a keyboard alone.
- **The app:** buttons, images and controls are labelled for screen readers.
  `[Automated checks of the labels arrive with #69 and #137.]`
- **By hand:** we test on real phones.

Automated checks can't catch everything, so we may still have issues we
don't know about.

### Known limitations

- **Checkout** is provided by Shopify. It's covered by Shopify's own
  accessibility commitments, not tested by us.
- **Product reviews** are provided by Judge.me.
- **Embedded content from other companies** may not meet the same standard.

### Tell us about a problem

If something is hard to use, or you need information in a different format,
email `[contact email]`. Tell us the page or screen and what happened, and
we'll reply within `[5]` working days.
